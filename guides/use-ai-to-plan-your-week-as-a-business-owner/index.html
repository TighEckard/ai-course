Plan Your Week as a Business Owner With AI

When you wear every hat, the urgent crowds out the important. AI helps you sort tasks, set priorities and protect time for what matters.

Step by step

  1. Dump everything. Type every task on your mind in any order.
  2. Add time estimates. Guess how long each takes. Rough is fine.
  3. Ask for a plan. Request a Monday to Friday schedule with breaks and a buffer for surprises.
  4. Mark the money tasks. Ask AI to put sales and customer work first.
  5. Review on Friday. Tell AI what was not done and ask it to adjust next week.

What to expect

Expect a realistic schedule with priority tasks and buffers.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not schedule every minute. Avoid ignoring recurring duties.

A good next step

Review on Friday.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Here is my task list: [paste]. I work Monday to Friday, 8 to 4, and I lose about an hour a day to interruptions. Build a realistic weekly schedule. Put customer-facing and money-making tasks first, and tell me what to drop.

Good to know. A plan only helps if it has slack. Ask AI to leave open time each day.

Common questions

What if I fall behind?

Paste your plan back in and ask AI to re-plan the rest of the week.

Can AI add things to my calendar?

Some assistants can connect to calendars, but you can also copy the plan in by hand.
