Handle a Refund Request Politely and Fairly With AI

Refund requests feel personal, but a calm, consistent reply keeps customers and protects your margins. AI can help you word a yes, a partial yes or a respectful no.

Step by step

  1. Decide your answer first. Check your written policy and the facts of the order.
  2. Share the facts with AI. Purchase date, item, reason given, and what you are willing to do.
  3. Ask for a kind, short reply. State the decision in the first two lines.
  4. Offer a next step. A refund, exchange, credit or repair.
  5. Send within a day. Quick replies calm most situations.

What to expect

Expect a short reply with the decision first, a brief reason and a clear next step. AI can offer a firm version, a generous version and a middle path so you can choose.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not argue about who is at fault, and do not reply while angry. Avoid promising things your policy does not allow. Do not share other customers' information.

A good next step

Write down what you decided so you treat the next similar request the same way.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

A customer asks for a refund 45 days after purchase. My policy allows returns within 30 days. Write a warm reply that explains the policy, offers a store credit of 50 percent as a goodwill gesture, and thanks them. Under 120 words.

Good to know. If a product might be unsafe or a customer alleges harm, stop and get advice before replying.

Common questions

Should I always say yes?

Not always, but one generous exception can win a loyal customer. Decide what you can afford.

Do I need a written policy?

Yes. Post it where customers buy.
