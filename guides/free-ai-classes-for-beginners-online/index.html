Free AI Classes for Beginners Online: 6 Ways to Start Today

You can learn to use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini without spending anything. Here are six free ways to start, what each is good for, and a simple one-week plan for beginners.

Six free ways to start learning AI

  1. Join a free live class. Riside offers a free first class on Zoom for adults, in a group of 12 or fewer, with no card needed. It is a low-pressure way to see how everything works before deciding anything.
  2. Use the free version of a chatbot. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini each have a free option. Free versions have usage limits and fewer features, but they are plenty for learning. See the best free AI tools for beginners.
  3. Ask your library. Many public libraries offer free technology help or classes. Call or stop by and ask whether anyone teaches AI tools.
  4. Read plain-English guides. Short guides with copy-and-paste prompts let you practice immediately. Start with what is ChatGPT.
  5. Ask the AI to teach you. A chatbot is a patient tutor. Tell it you are a beginner and ask for one small lesson at a time.
  6. Practice on a real task. Pick something you need to do anyway, such as a thank-you note, a grocery list or a letter to a landlord, and let AI help.

A simple one-week plan

DayWhat to try (10 to 15 minutes)
Day 1Open a free chatbot and ask it what it can help with.
Day 2Ask it to explain something you have always wondered about, in plain words.
Day 3Have it help write a short email or note you actually need to send.
Day 4Practice follow-up questions: ask it to make the answer shorter, warmer or simpler.
Day 5Plan something real: a meal plan, a day trip or a weekend to-do list.
Day 6Test it on a message you are unsure about, as a second opinion on whether it could be a scam.
Day 7Review what worked, and write down your three favorite prompts.

A first prompt to try

Copy and paste

I am a complete beginner. Teach me one useful thing about using AI today in under 150 words, then give me one small exercise to try.

What free does not give you

Free options are great for getting started. What they usually do not give you is a person who watches what you are doing and answers your question right then. That is where a live class helps. If you get stuck, you can ask and get an answer that fits you.

Good to know. Whatever you use, never type passwords, bank numbers or full medical records into a chatbot. Our guide what never to type into an AI chatbot has the complete list.
