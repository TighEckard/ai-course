Brain Games, Trivia and Puzzles With AI

Keeping your mind active is enjoyable when the games are about things you love.

Step by step

  1. Pick your topic. Movies from the 1970s, birds, baseball.
  2. Ask for a quiz. Ten questions, one at a time.
  3. Set the difficulty. Easy to tricky.
  4. Ask for hints. Not answers.
  5. Play with someone. Family quiz night.

What to expect

Expect quizzes on your favorite topics at the level you choose.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not trust every answer. Avoid long sessions that tire you.

A good next step

Invite a friend to play.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Quiz me on classic movies of the 1960s. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, then tell me if I am right and share a fun fact. Start easy.

Good to know. Trivia answers can be wrong. If you disagree, look it up.
