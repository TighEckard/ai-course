Write Poetry With AI as a Partner

Poetry is personal. AI can offer forms, rhymes and feedback, while you supply the feeling.

Step by step

  1. Pick a subject. A garden, a memory.
  2. Choose a form. Haiku, limerick, free verse.
  3. Ask for a few lines.
  4. Rewrite in your voice.
  5. Share it.

What to expect

Expect AI to teach forms, suggest rhymes and offer feedback on rhythm. It can also act as a gentle editor who asks what you meant by a line.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not accept the first draft as your poem. AI verse tends to be generic, with stock images. Add a detail only you know, like the smell of your mother's kitchen. Avoid claiming AI work as your own if you share it in a contest.

A good next step

Read your poem aloud and change any line that feels stiff. Share it with a friend or a library poetry group.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Teach me the haiku form and give me three examples about autumn. Then ask me to write my own.

Good to know. Poems you write remain yours. Do not pretend an AI poem is yours.
