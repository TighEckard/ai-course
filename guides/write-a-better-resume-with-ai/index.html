Improve Your Resume With AI

AI can turn vague duties into clear achievements. The facts must always be true and yours.

Step by step

  1. Paste a job description. Pick a role you really want.
  2. Paste your resume text. Remove your address and phone number first.
  3. Ask for stronger bullets. Request action verbs and measurable results where you have them.
  4. Add real numbers. Fill in actual figures yourself.
  5. Proofread. Check dates, titles and spelling.

What to expect

Expect stronger bullets.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not invent. Avoid keyword stuffing.

A good next step

Proofread.

Before you start: gather the raw material

  • A list of every job you have held, with dates and employers.
  • Two or three accomplishments per job, ideally with numbers: people managed, money saved, customers served.
  • The job posting you are applying to, copied into a document.
  • Your education, licenses and training.

Tailor it to one job, step by step

  1. Paste the job posting. Tell the assistant: "Here is a job posting. List the ten skills and keywords it cares about most."
  2. Paste your current resume. Ask: "Which of these skills do I already show, and which are missing or buried?"
  3. Rewrite the top third. Ask for a short summary and a skills list that uses the posting's own words, but only where it is true for you.
  4. Turn duties into results. Give it "ran the front desk" and ask for a stronger line. Then add your real numbers, such as "greeted about 80 visitors a day."
  5. Read every line aloud. Delete anything you could not explain in an interview.

Before and after

  1. Before. "Responsible for managing a team and handling customer complaints."
  2. After. "Led a team of 6 and resolved customer complaints within one business day, raising satisfaction scores."
  3. The rule. Only keep a number if it is true. Replace guesses with honest estimates such as "about" or "more than."

Handling an age or a gap

  • Focus on the last 10 to 15 years of experience unless older work is directly relevant.
  • Leave graduation years off if you prefer.
  • Describe a gap honestly in one line, such as caregiving, health or retraining.
  • List current skills such as video meetings, spreadsheets and AI tools.

Final checks

  • Keep it to one or two pages.
  • Use a plain layout. Fancy columns and graphics can confuse the software that scans resumes.
  • Save as a PDF unless the employer asks for another format.
  • Ask a friend to proofread it.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Here is a job description: [paste]. Here are my current resume bullets: [paste]. Rewrite the bullets to match the job where my experience truly fits. Do not invent any skills, titles or numbers. Mark any place where I should add a real figure.

Good to know. Employers verify facts. A made-up claim can cost you the job later.

Common questions

Will AI-written resumes get filtered?

Clear, honest, relevant resumes do best. Avoid stuffing keywords.

How long should it be?

One page for most people, two if you have long experience.

Will employers know I used AI?

Employers care that the resume is accurate and clear. Use AI to polish your own facts, never to invent experience.

Should I put my full address?

City and state are usually enough. Add a phone number and a professional email.

Can AI write a cover letter too?

Yes. Give it the posting and three true points about you, then edit the draft in your own voice.

Is it safe to paste my resume in?

Remove your street address and any ID numbers before pasting.
