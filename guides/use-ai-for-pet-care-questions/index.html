Use AI for Pet Care Questions (and Know When to Call the Vet)

Pets are family. AI can help you prepare for a vet visit and plan routines, but cannot examine your animal.

Step by step

  1. Describe your pet. Species, age, weight.
  2. Ask for routines. Feeding and exercise ideas.
  3. List symptoms for the vet. Dates and changes.
  4. Call the vet for health issues. Especially sudden changes.
  5. Check food safety. Confirm with your vet.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

My 9 year old beagle seems less interested in walks. Make a list of questions to ask my vet and a short log I can keep for a week to bring to the appointment.

Good to know. For poisoning or emergencies, call your vet or an animal poison hotline right away.
