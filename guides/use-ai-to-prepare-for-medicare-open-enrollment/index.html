Prepare for Medicare Open Enrollment With AI

Each fall, many people review their plan. AI can explain the terms and help you prepare questions.

Step by step

  1. List your needs. Doctors, medications and budget.
  2. Ask for plain definitions. Premium, deductible, copay.
  3. Make a comparison table. Plan A versus Plan B.
  4. Use the official plan finder. And free counselors.
  5. Decide before the deadline. Dates matter.

What to expect

Expect a comparison table and a checklist of doctors, medicines and costs to verify in the official plan finder.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not rely on a chatbot for plan details. Avoid sharing your Medicare number with callers. Do not wait until the last day to decide.

A good next step

Book a free appointment with your state health insurance counselor before the deadline.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Give me a checklist to compare two Medicare drug plans, including questions about my doctors and my regular medications. Put it in a table.

Good to know. Free counseling is available through your state health insurance program.
