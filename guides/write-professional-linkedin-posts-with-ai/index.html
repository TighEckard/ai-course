Write LinkedIn Posts That Sound Like You

Posting regularly builds credibility. AI helps shape your story.

Step by step

  1. Choose a story.
  2. Draft.
  3. Remove buzzwords.
  4. Add a question.
  5. Post.

What to expect

Expect a post with a hook, a short story and a question, in a conversational tone.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not claim results you did not achieve. Avoid hashtags stuffing. Do not post confidential client details.

A good next step

Post once a week for a month and note which posts get replies.

A worked example

A nurse manager shares a story about mentoring a new graduate. AI makes it a short post with a clear opening, one lesson and a question. He removes a line about an actual patient, adds his own closing and posts it. Colleagues comment with their own stories.

Post ideas

  • Lessons from a project.
  • A short story from your week.
  • Something you learned.
  • Congratulating someone.

Write one, step by step

  1. Pick one idea.
  2. Write rough notes.
  3. Ask for a 100-word draft.
  4. Add your voice.
  5. End with a question.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Turn this experience into a short LinkedIn post, plain and personal: [story].

Good to know. Authenticity beats polish.
