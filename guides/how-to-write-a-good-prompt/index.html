How to Write a Good AI Prompt (With Examples)

A prompt is simply what you type to the AI. The secret to good answers is not clever tricks. It is giving the AI the same details you would give a person helping you. Here is a simple pattern that works almost every time.

Step by step

  1. Say who you are and what you need. Start with a sentence of context. For example, I am retired and planning a trip, or I am writing to my landlord.
  2. Say what you want back. A short email, a list, a table, three options, a step-by-step plan. If you do not say, you get whatever the AI picks.
  3. Add the details that matter. Dates, budget, tone, who it is for, what you already tried. More detail means a more useful answer.
  4. Ask for the style you like. Try plain words, short sentences, friendly but professional, or no jargon.
  5. Follow up instead of starting over. If the answer is close, say make it shorter, make it warmer, or explain step two again. The AI remembers the conversation.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

I am a retired teacher writing to my neighbor about a fence that crosses our property line. Write a polite, firm letter of about 150 words. Keep it friendly and suggest we talk this week.

Good to know. If you are stuck, ask the AI itself: What else do you need to know to help me with this?

Common questions

Do I need to say please?

It does not matter to the AI, so use whatever feels natural.

How long should a prompt be?

As long as it takes to include the details that matter. Three or four sentences is common.
