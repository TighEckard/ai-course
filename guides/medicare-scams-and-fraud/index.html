Medicare Scams: What to Know Before You Share Your Number

Your Medicare number is valuable to thieves. Real Medicare does not cold call to sell you something.

Step by step

  1. Guard your card. Treat it like a credit card.
  2. Hang up on offers. Free braces and tests.
  3. Check your statements. Look for services you did not receive.
  4. Use trusted helpers. Your State Health Insurance Assistance Program is free.
  5. Report fraud. Call the Medicare line.

What to expect

Expect calls about free medical equipment, genetic tests or a new Medicare card, often during open enrollment. Callers ask for your Medicare number, which they use to bill for services you never received.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not give out your number to anyone who contacted you. Do not accept free items that require your number. Do not ignore statements showing services you did not get.

A good next step

Review your statements monthly and call the official Medicare line or your state health insurance program with questions.
