Create a FAQ Page for Your Business With AI

If you answer the same questions all week, a FAQ page can do some of that work for you. AI helps you organize and word the answers.

Step by step

  1. Collect real questions. Look at emails, texts and voicemails from the past month.
  2. List the true answers. Write each answer briefly in your own words, with prices and policies.
  3. Ask AI to tidy. Paste your list and ask for clear question and answer pairs.
  4. Add the page to your site or Facebook. Place it where customers already look.
  5. Update every season. Review it when prices or hours change.

What to expect

Expect a tidy list of questions and answers in your words.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not publish answers you have not checked. Avoid long answers.

A good next step

Add the FAQ to your website or Facebook page.

Where the questions come from

  • Emails and calls you get again and again.
  • Messages and comments on social media.
  • What new customers ask during the first visit.

Build it, step by step

  1. List the questions. Write ten to fifteen in your own words.
  2. Draft answers. Give your facts, such as prices, hours and policies, and ask for short, friendly answers.
  3. Check every fact. Never publish an answer you have not verified.
  4. Group them. Ask the assistant to sort into topics like ordering, pricing and returns.
  5. Publish and update. Review the list every few months.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Here are questions customers ask my [business], with my rough answers. Turn them into a clean FAQ with clear headings. Keep my facts exactly as written and do not add new policies. [paste list]

Good to know. Make sure hours, prices and policies on your FAQ match everywhere else.

Common questions

How many questions are enough?

Eight to twelve good ones cover most needs.

Does a FAQ help get found online?

Clear question and answer pages are useful to visitors and can help search engines understand your page.

Where do I put it?

On your website, in your shop and as a printed sheet.

How many questions do I need?

Ten good ones beat fifty weak ones.

Can AI answer customers directly?

You can add a chat tool later. Start with a clear FAQ.
