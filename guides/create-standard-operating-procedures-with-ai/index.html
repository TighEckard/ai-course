Write Simple How-To Procedures for Your Business

Writing down a process frees you from explaining it again. AI turns spoken steps into a checklist.

Step by step

  1. Choose one process.
  2. Talk through the steps.
  3. Ask for a checklist.
  4. Test it with someone new.
  5. Store it where staff can find it.

What to expect

Expect numbered steps, a short list of materials and a note for when something goes wrong, written so a new person can follow it on day one.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not write a manual nobody reads. Keep each procedure to a page. Avoid skipping the why, because staff follow steps better when they know the reason.

A good next step

Ask a new or part-time worker to try a procedure and mark anything unclear.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Turn these spoken notes into a clear numbered procedure for opening the shop each morning: [notes].
