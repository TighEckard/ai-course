Write Clear Project Status Reports With AI

Good reports are short and honest.

Step by step

  1. Gather updates.
  2. Group by progress, risks, needs.
  3. Draft.
  4. Add dates.
  5. Send.

What to expect

Expect a one-page structure that highlights decisions needed.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not hide risks. Avoid jargon.

A good next step

Send at the same time each week.

A worked example

A project lead pastes bullet notes: website draft done, photos delayed a week, budget on track. AI returns a one-page report with three headings, flags the photo delay as the main risk, and adds a line asking the manager to approve a new date. The lead changes one date, adds the actual budget figure and sends it in five minutes instead of thirty.

A good status report has

  • Where things stand: on track, at risk or late.
  • What was done.
  • What is next.
  • Risks and what you need from others.

Write one, step by step

  1. Gather the facts.
  2. Paste them in. Ask for a half-page summary.
  3. Lead with the headline.
  4. Check dates and names.
  5. Send on a regular schedule.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Turn these notes into a one page project status report with sections for progress, risks and needs: [notes].

Good to know. Avoid confidential details in public tools.
