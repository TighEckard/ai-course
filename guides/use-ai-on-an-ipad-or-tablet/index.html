Use AI on an iPad or Tablet

Tablets are great for AI because the text is big and the keyboard is roomy. Setup takes minutes.

Step by step

  1. Download the app or open the site. Use the app store or the assistant's website.
  2. Increase the text size. In Settings, choose Display and Text Size.
  3. Use dictation. Tap the microphone on the keyboard.
  4. Use a stand. Prop the tablet at eye level.
  5. Take breaks. Rest your eyes and wrists.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Please write your answers in short paragraphs with plenty of line breaks, so they are easy to read on a tablet.

Good to know. A tablet with a keyboard case makes longer writing easier.
