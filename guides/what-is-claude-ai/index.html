What Is Claude AI? A Plain-English Introduction

Claude is an AI assistant made by a company called Anthropic. You type or speak a question and it answers in conversation, much like ChatGPT or Gemini. People often use it for writing, summarizing long documents and talking through problems.

Step by step

  1. Go to the official site. Type claude.ai yourself rather than clicking an ad.
  2. Create an account. An email address is usually enough to start.
  3. Ask a simple first question. For example: explain what you can help me with.
  4. Try a real task. Paste a letter and ask for a plain summary, with private details removed.
  5. Give feedback. Say shorter, simpler or more formal, and it adjusts.

What to expect

Expect a friendly, careful assistant that is good at explaining and at working with long text. You can paste a document and ask for the main points, or ask it to rewrite something more plainly.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not treat it as an expert in medicine, law or taxes. Avoid giving it passwords or account numbers. Do not assume its answers are always right.

A good next step

Ask the same question to Claude and another assistant and compare.

What Claude is good at

  • Careful writing, such as letters, summaries and edits that keep your own voice.
  • Reading long documents you paste or upload and answering questions about them.
  • Thinking through a decision by laying out pros, cons and questions to ask.
  • Explaining difficult topics at the level you choose.
  • Helping with spreadsheets, simple code and planning.

Getting started, step by step

  1. Go to claude.ai. Create an account with your email address and follow the prompts. The phone app is available in the usual app stores.
  2. Type your request. For example: "Help me write a kind but clear note to my neighbor about the fence."
  3. Upload a document if you like. Use the attach button to add a PDF or photo, then ask "What are the three most important points?"
  4. Refine. Ask for a shorter version, a warmer tone or a list of questions you should ask.

A worked example

  1. Your request. "Here is a letter from my insurance company. Explain what it means in plain words and list what I need to do and by when." Then paste the text.
  2. What good help looks like. A short summary, a checklist of next steps and a note about anything it could not tell from the letter.
  3. Your next step. Confirm deadlines and amounts with the insurer by calling the number printed on your card.

Limits to know

  • Like every assistant, it can make mistakes, so check key facts.
  • It does not replace professional advice for medical, legal or financial decisions.
  • Free use usually has message limits that reset over time.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Hello. I am new to AI. Explain in simple words what you can help me with, and give me three easy things to try today.

Good to know. Plans, limits and features change. Check the company's site for what is free today.

Common questions

Is Claude free?

There is a free version with limits. Paid plans add more usage.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

They are similar. Try both on the same question and keep the one you like.

Who makes Claude?

Claude is made by Anthropic, an AI safety company.

Is it different from ChatGPT?

Both are AI assistants. They write a bit differently and have different features, so many people try both on the same question.

Is my conversation private?

Read the privacy settings and policy, and avoid typing sensitive numbers or passwords.

Can I talk to it instead of typing?

The phone apps usually support dictation or voice, depending on your device and plan.
