Using AI to Prepare for Medicare Decisions

Medicare has a lot of vocabulary: Part A, Part B, Advantage, Medigap, formulary. AI can explain the terms in plain words and help you get ready to talk to an official source or a licensed counselor.

Step by step

  1. Ask what each term means. Explain Part A, Part B, Part D and Medicare Advantage in simple words.
  2. List your needs. Doctors you want to keep, medicines you take, how often you travel.
  3. Ask for questions to ask. Which questions should I ask a plan before enrolling?
  4. Check official sources. Medicare.gov and your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) give free, unbiased help.
  5. Watch for pressure. Be cautious of unsolicited calls or anyone rushing you to enroll.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Explain the difference between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage in plain words, then list ten questions I should ask before choosing. I am not asking for a recommendation.

Good to know. Plan details change every year. Confirm costs and coverage on Medicare.gov before you decide.

Common questions

Can AI pick my plan?

It should not. Rules and prices vary and change, so use official tools or a licensed counselor.

Is SHIP free?

Yes, SHIP counselors offer free help in every state.
