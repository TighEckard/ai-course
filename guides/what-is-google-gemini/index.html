What Is Google Gemini? A Plain-English Introduction

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. It can answer questions, help you write and, with your permission, work with other Google tools. If you already use Gmail or Google Maps, it may feel familiar.

Step by step

  1. Open it. Go to gemini.google.com or find the app on your phone.
  2. Sign in with a Google account. You may already have one.
  3. Ask a question in plain words. Like you would ask a friend.
  4. Check the answer. Use Google Search or the linked sources for important facts.
  5. Review privacy settings. Choose what activity is saved.

What to expect

Expect quick answers, drafts and ideas. In the app on a phone it can also respond to voice requests.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not connect more of your accounts than you need. Avoid trusting a single answer for money or health decisions.

A good next step

Try a travel-planning question and compare with a regular search.

What Gemini does

  • Answers questions and drafts text like other AI assistants.
  • Works with Google tools you may already use, such as Gmail, Docs and Maps, depending on your account and settings.
  • Can describe a photo you take and answer questions about it.
  • Helps plan trips, compare products and summarize long pages.

Getting started, step by step

  1. Sign in. Go to gemini.google.com with your Google account, or open the Gemini app on your phone.
  2. Ask something specific. "Plan a gentle three-day visit to Savannah for two people who prefer not to walk far."
  3. Ask for changes. "Swap the second day for indoor activities."
  4. Use it with your photos. Upload a picture of a plant or a label and ask what it is.
  5. Review your settings. Open the activity controls and decide what you want saved.

When to use it versus a search

  • Use a search when you want an official page, a phone number or a store hours listing.
  • Use Gemini when you want an explanation, a draft or a plan.
  • For anything that matters, open the source it points to and read it.

A short tour of what you can do

  1. Ask and plan. "Plan a gentle weekend in Santa Fe. We like museums and dislike long walks."
  2. Understand a photo. Take a picture of a medicine label or a confusing sign and ask what it says.
  3. Summarize. Paste a long article and ask for five bullet points.
  4. Compare. Ask for a table comparing two phone plans, then check the details on the company sites.
  5. Write. Ask for a thank-you note or a polite complaint and edit it.

Gemini with your Google account

  • If you turn on connections, Gemini can help with things in Gmail, Docs, Calendar and Maps.
  • You control which apps it can use in your settings.
  • Be careful with sensitive emails and documents, and check what activity is saved.
  • You can review and delete your activity from your Google account.

Getting better answers

  • Tell it who you are and what you want.
  • Give it the facts it needs, such as dates and places.
  • Ask it to explain its reasoning or list what to verify.
  • Ask for sources and open them.
  • If an answer looks off, say so and ask it to try again.

When not to trust it

  • Medical, legal and financial decisions.
  • Anything where the date, price or rule has changed recently.
  • Exact quotes or sources you have not verified.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Plan a relaxed day trip near [your town] for two adults who prefer quiet places. Include a lunch idea and remind me to check opening hours.

Good to know. Connecting AI to your email or files is optional. Only switch on connections you understand.

Common questions

Do I need a Google account?

Usually yes for the full experience.

Is it the same as Google Search?

No. Search finds web pages. Gemini writes answers and helps with tasks.

Is Gemini free?

There is usually a free version and paid options with extra features. Check the current plans.

Does it read my email?

Only if you turn on features that connect it to your Google apps. Review those settings.

Is it accurate?

Often, but it can be wrong. Verify important facts.

How is Gemini different from Google Search?

Search finds pages. Gemini writes an answer or draft and may use search to inform it.

Do I need to pay?

There is a free version. Paid plans add extras. Check the current options.
