AI for Sales and Customer Service Roles

Sales and service jobs involve many messages. AI helps with prep and wording.

Step by step

  1. Prepare before the call. Ask for questions to ask and likely objections.
  2. Write the follow-up. Summarize what was agreed.
  3. Calm an upset customer. Ask for an empathetic reply that offers a next step.
  4. Role-play. Practice difficult conversations.
  5. Respect privacy. Keep customer details out of public tools.

What to expect

Expect call prep and follow-up drafts.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not share customer data. Avoid pushiness.

A good next step

Practice objections.

A prompt you can copy

Type this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Help me prepare for a call with a prospect in [industry]. Give me five open-ended questions to ask, three likely objections, and a polite way to respond to each.

Good to know. Follow your company rules on customer data and approved tools.
