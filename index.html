Privacy

Plain-language summary. Last updated October 26, 2026.

What we collect

When you reserve a free class we collect your first name, email address and the class time you chose. When you create a member account, we store your email and a password (kept scrambled, never readable by us).

How we use it

We use it to send class details and run your membership. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. Your card or bank details are entered on paypal.com and are never seen or stored by us. We receive your name, email and payment status from PayPal so we can give you member access.

Who sees it

Our hosting and database provider stores your details securely for us. Zoom processes your name and email when you join a class. Class recordings may include your voice or image if you speak or turn on your camera.

Your choices

Email us at hello@aicoffeeclub.com to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop receiving class emails. We reply within a few days.

We will never

Ask for your passwords, bank details or card number by email, phone or in class.

Terms

Plain-language terms. Last updated October 26, 2026.

Free first class

Your first class is free. No card is needed and there is no obligation to join.

Membership

Membership is $46 USD per month, billed monthly through PayPal until you cancel. It includes the weekly live classes and access to recordings and how-to sheets.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your PayPal account, or email us and we will cancel it for you. Cancelling stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the month you have paid for. We do not give refunds for part of a month.

Classes

Classes are live on Zoom with small groups. Times can change, and we will tell you by email. If we cancel a class, we will offer another one.

Using AI

Classes teach general skills. AI tools can make mistakes, so please check important answers, especially about health, money or legal matters. Nothing taught here is professional advice.

Being kind

Please be respectful to teachers and other members. We may remove anyone who is not, with a refund of the current month.

Recordings and your account

Recordings and sheets are for your own use and may not be shared or sold. Your sign-in is for you alone.
